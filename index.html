Who we are

International perspective. Practical execution.

Nova Frontera Limited is a Hong Kong-incorporated trading company focused on connecting suppliers and business customers across international markets. Our business activities include sourcing, procurement and wholesale distribution of agricultural commodities and food-related products, supported by independent logistics partners.
